My 10-Step Claude System to Grow on Facebook in 2025

Short answer: For a long time I posted on Facebook without a system. I posted when I felt like it, about whatever came to mind, and then I wondered why my reach stayed flat. My 10-step Claude system changed that.

For a long time I posted on Facebook without a system. I posted when I felt like it, about whatever came to mind, and then I wondered why my reach stayed flat. My 10-step Claude system changed that. I now run every page through the same ten steps: find the niche, plan 30 days, write hooks, generate video ideas, write captions, fix the bio, study the winners, monetize, audit what is not working, and build authority. No step is skipped, and no step is done out of order. Here is my system, exactly as I use it.

Step 1: My Niche Comes First

My first step is never posting — it is choosing. I ask Claude to act as my growth strategist and find me a content angle with high audience demand but low competition. My inputs are my interests, my skills, and my audience's pain points. My output is five specific niche ideas, ranked from highest to lowest growth potential, each with its audience, why it works, and how I should start. If my niche is wrong, my next nine steps are wasted, so I never rush this one.

Step 2: My 30-Day Content Plan

Once my niche is set, I build my 30-day plan. For each day I want four things: the content idea, the format, the hook, and the call to action. My mix is fixed: educational posts, engaging posts, viral-style posts, personal stories, and promotional posts. My tone stays consistent across all thirty days. This plan is why I never sit down and wonder what to post — my month is decided before my month begins.

Step 3: My Hooks in the First 3 Seconds

My hook rule is brutal: if the first 3 seconds do not grab attention, the rest of my post does not exist. I generate twenty hooks at a time, built on curiosity, controversy, surprise, emotion, and bold statements. Then I rank them from strongest to weakest and study why each one works. My best hook goes on my best post — I never waste a strong hook on weak content.

Step 4: My 30 Video Ideas, Sorted by Feeling

Video is where my page grows fastest, so I never generate video ideas one at a time. I generate thirty at once, divided evenly: ten educational, ten entertaining, and ten emotional or relatable. For each idea I want the title, the hook, and the basic script structure. My educational videos teach, my entertaining videos get shared, and my emotional videos get comments. All three feelings, every month.

Step 5: My Caption Formula

My caption formula has five parts, and I never skip one. A strong hook in the first line. Emotional storytelling in the middle. Short, easy-to-read lines — never a wall of text. A curiosity gap or a bold opinion. And a call to action that asks for comments and shares. My tone stays human and relatable, never robotic. A caption is not a description of my post; it is the second hook.

Step 6: My Bio in One Glance

My bio has one job: a stranger should understand my page in five seconds. What my page is about, who it is for, why they should follow, and what value they will get. I generate five bio options and pick the clearest, most memorable, most professional one. My bio is not a place for cleverness — it is a place for clarity, because clarity is what turns a visitor into a follower.

Step 7: My Competitor Study — Adapt, Never Copy

Every month I study the successful pages in my niche. I break down what gets the most engagement, their strongest hook patterns, the topics they repeat, the emotional triggers behind their viral posts, and the post structures that generate comments and shares. My rule is strict: I adapt the pattern, I never copy the content. What makes them different is what I learn from — and what I can do better is where my strategy comes from.

Step 8: My Monetization Plan — Trust Before Selling

I do not monetize on day one. My monetization plan covers digital products, affiliate opportunities, sponsorships, services I can offer, lead generation, and content that naturally promotes products. But my order is fixed: build trust first, sell second. My plan tells me what to focus on first, what to avoid, and how my income grows gradually as my page grows. An audience that trusts me buys from me; an audience that feels sold to leaves me.

Step 9: My Audit When Reach Drops

When my reach drops, I do not panic-post. I audit. I check my hook quality, my topic selection, my audience targeting, my content format, my post structure, my engagement patterns, my posting consistency, my call-to-action quality, and my value and shareability. I find the biggest problem first, then I make a step-by-step fix plan: what I change, what I stop doing, and what I start posting. My audit turns a bad week into a fixable list.

Step 10: My Authority Brand for the Long Term

My final step is the one most creators skip: authority. My content strategy mixes educational content, useful tips, storytelling, personal experiences, trust-building posts, strong opinions, audience questions, and content that proves my expertise. I build a loyal audience that trusts my content instead of chasing viral views. Viral views fade in a day; authority compounds for years. My goal was never one viral post — it was a page people trust.

My Advice: Run the Steps in Order

My biggest mistake used to be jumping to step eight before step one. Do not monetize a page with no niche, no plan, and no trust. Run my ten steps in order, one at a time, and let each step make the next one easier. That is my system, and it is the reason my Facebook growth finally stopped being random.
