Stop Paying for AI Video Prompts. I Reverse-Engineered the Exact System Top Creators Use.

Short answer: I never pay for AI video prompts. My secret method is simple: every viral video already contains its own prompt — in its shots, its pacing, its lighting, and its movement. I just extract it.

I never pay for AI video prompts. My secret method is simple: every viral video already contains its own prompt — in its shots, its pacing, its lighting, and its movement. I just extract it. Here is my three-step workflow.

1. My First Step: I Grab the Video I Want to Learn From

I find a video I want to learn from and download it. My tool for this is Publer's free video downloader — it pulls from any public profile, on web or mobile. I'm not stealing the video — I'm studying it, the way an editor studies a great cut.

2. My Trick: I Make AI Watch It Frame by Frame

I upload the clip to Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini and ask it to watch the video frame by frame, then write me a prompt built specifically for Seedance 2.5 that recreates it. My frame-by-frame instruction matters — it forces the model to notice camera movement, transitions, and timing instead of guessing from a thumbnail. My optional twist: I upload my own photo and ask the model to swap the video's character with me.

3. My Final Step: I Generate and Publish

I copy the prompt it gives me and generate the video. My tool here is Syllaby — it puts all the major video models in one place, with scheduling and publishing built in — or, if I work inside Claude or ChatGPT, I connect a video-model MCP and generate without leaving the chat.

My Rule Before I Run This

A frame-by-frame recreation is a study exercise. My rule is simple: I learn the structure — the hook, the pacing, the payoff — and then I build something of my own on top of it. I use my workflow to learn faster, then I make the idea mine.
