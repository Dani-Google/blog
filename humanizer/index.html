Stop Pasting Robotic ChatGPT Text. This Free Humanizer Makes It Sound Human.

Short answer: I can spot ChatGPT text in seconds. So can everyone else now — the stiff sentences, the perfect structure, the words no human would actually say. My fix is not to hide the AI.

I can spot ChatGPT text in seconds. So can everyone else now — the stiff sentences, the perfect structure, the words no human would actually say. My fix is not to hide the AI. My fix is a set of eight prompts I run on my drafts until they read like something I actually wrote. Here they are, in the order I use them.

1. My Professional Humanizer

My first prompt is the workhorse. I hand my draft to Claude or ChatGPT as a professional human-writing editor and ask for a natural, conversational rewrite — robotic phrasing out, stiff structure out, unnatural flow out, meaning untouched.

Prompt: Act as my professional human-writing editor. Rewrite this text so it sounds natural, human, and conversational. Remove robotic phrasing, stiff structure, and unnatural flow, but keep the original meaning exactly intact. Text: [paste text]

2. My Natural Tone Converter

My second prompt fixes the voice. I ask for the tone of a real human with experience and confidence — better rhythm, better word choice, better flow, nothing robotic, nothing stiffly formal.

Prompt: Rewrite this text in the tone of a real human with experience and confidence. Improve the rhythm, the word choice, and the flow, and remove anything robotic or overly formal. Text: [paste text]

3. My Detection-Safe Rewrite

My third prompt is about feel. I ask for text that does not feel AI-generated: varied sentence lengths, natural phrasing, smooth transitions — organic and authentic, with no fluff added.

Prompt: Humanize this text so it does not feel AI-generated. Vary the sentence lengths, use natural phrasing, improve the transitions, and keep it organic and authentic without adding fluff. Text: [paste text]

4. My Conversational Rewrite

My fourth prompt makes it warm. Conversational, natural, human — long sentences broken up, complex phrases simplified, AI-like stiffness removed.

Prompt: Rewrite this text to be conversational, warm, and natural. Break up long sentences, simplify complex phrases, and remove any AI-like stiffness. Text: [paste text]

5. My Emotion and Flow Humanizer

My fifth prompt adds the human touch: subtle emotion, smooth flow, natural emphasis. Monotone is what gives AI away — this prompt kills the monotone.

Prompt: Add subtle human emotion, smooth flow, and natural emphasis to this text. Remove any monotone phrasing. Text: [paste text]

6. My Simple Language Converter

My sixth prompt strips it down. Simple, natural human language — jargon out, stiff phrasing out. If a sentence sounds like a press release, this prompt rewrites it like a person.

Prompt: Rewrite this text in simple, natural human language. Remove jargon and any stiff or AI-like phrasing. Text: [paste text]

7. My Rhythm and Style Fixer

My seventh prompt fixes the music of the text. Natural human rhythm and style — varied sentence lengths, adjusted pacing, repetitive and predictable phrasing removed. Humans don't write in perfect patterns; this prompt breaks the pattern.

Prompt: Fix the rhythm and style of this text so it reads like natural human writing. Vary the sentence lengths, adjust the pacing, and remove repetitive or predictable phrasing. Text: [paste text]

8. My Final Master Edit

My eighth prompt is the finish line. I hand over the draft and ask for a fully human-sounding final version — tone, flow, phrasing, and structure all fixed, so it reads natural, confident, and written by a real person. This is the version I publish.

Prompt: Take this draft and transform it into a fully human-sounding final version. Fix the tone, flow, phrasing, and structure so it feels natural, confident, and written by a real person. Draft: [paste text]

My Rule Before You Run These

My rule before you run these: I use them to polish my own drafts — to make my own thinking read the way I would actually say it. I do not use them to pass off work that is not mine. The prompts make text human; only you can make it honest.
