How I Audit and Rebuild Your Instagram Brand With Claude

Short answer: Most creators ask AI for one caption at a time. That's the wrong job. My secret method is different: I hand Claude my entire Instagram page, and it audits and rebuilds it the way a professional agency would — profile, content, reels, engagement, and sales.

Most creators ask AI for one caption at a time. That's the wrong job. My secret method is different: I hand Claude my entire Instagram page, and it audits and rebuilds it the way a professional agency would — profile, content, reels, engagement, and sales. Below are my six prompts, in the exact order I use them.

1. My Full Instagram Audit Prompt

My first step, always. I hand Claude my whole page and ask it to think like a growth expert, not a caption machine.

Act as an Instagram growth expert. Audit my profile, bio, content, and positioning. Tell me exactly what is holding my growth back, then give me a step-by-step plan to fix it, in order of impact.

2. My 30 Viral Content Ideas

Once my audit shows what is broken, I need ideas that actually travel. Replace [NICHE] with yours.

My niche is [NICHE]. Give me 30 Instagram content ideas with the highest potential for views, shares, saves, and new followers. For each idea, add one line on why it would work.

3. My 30-Day Reels Plan

Ideas are useless without a schedule. This is my prompt that turns them into a month of reels.

Build me a 30-day Instagram Reels plan for [NICHE]. For each day, give me the hook, the video idea, a caption, and a strong call to action.

4. My Bio Makeover

This is where most people lose followers — the bio. If a visitor cannot tell in three seconds who you help and why they should follow, they leave. My bio prompt fixes that.

Rewrite my Instagram bio so it instantly says who I help, what value I give, and why someone should follow me. Give me 5 versions, each with a different angle.

5. My Engagement Growth System

Followers mean nothing if nobody talks back. This is my system for the daily habits that turn visitors into loyal followers.

Design an Instagram engagement strategy that brings in my ideal audience and turns profile visitors into loyal followers. Include what I should do daily, weekly, and in comments and DMs.

6. My Sales Funnel

Attention without income is a hobby. My last prompt turns views into leads and paying customers, without sounding pushy.

Build an Instagram sales funnel for my [PRODUCT/SERVICE] that turns content views into leads and paying customers, without sounding pushy or salesy.

This is my system, and I run it in this order: my audit first, then my bio, then my ideas, then my reels plan, then my engagement, then my sales funnel. And two rules I follow before I run any of them. First, my audit is only as good as the numbers I feed it — I paste real data (reach, saves, shares, profile visits), not vibes. Second, I treat the output as a draft, not a verdict. I still know my audience better than any model does.
